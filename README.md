# Multimedia in Librarianship / Multimedia in Records and Archives Management

**Two bachelor's degree courses, one shared multimedia learning website**

| Programme | Course |
|---|---|
| Bachelor of Library and Information Studies (BLISS III) | **LIU08106 — Multimedia in Librarianship** |
| Bachelor of Records and Information Management (BRIM III) | **RIU08106 — Multimedia in Records and Archives Management** |

## About the course

This 12-unit course introduces the fundamentals of multimedia and their application to libraries, records offices and archives. Both programmes study the same core material, with separate practical examples and application assignments where appropriate. The materials are student-facing and may be read independently.

## Units

1. Introduction to Multimedia and Professional Applications
2. Digital Media Fundamentals and Visual Communication
3. Digital Images and Image Editing
4. Audio in Multimedia Systems
5. Video, Animation and Multimedia Storytelling
6. File Formats, Compression and Delivery
7. Multimedia Authoring, Hypermedia and Interaction
8. Digitisation Planning and Quality Control
9. Multimedia Metadata, Organisation and Retrieval
10. Digital Preservation, Security and Continuity
11. Legal, Ethical and Inclusive Multimedia Practice
12. Multimedia Publishing, Evaluation and Applied Project

## Learning materials

Each unit provides printable notes, a downloadable PDF and editable PowerPoint, interactive or guided practice, review questions, a formative quiz and an applied assignment. A beginners' glossary is included. Example data and media are fictional or synthetic unless explicitly stated otherwise.
