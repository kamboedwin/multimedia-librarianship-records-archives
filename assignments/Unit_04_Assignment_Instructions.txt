UNIT 04 — AUDIO IN MULTIMEDIA SYSTEMS
LIU08106 (BLISS III) / RIU08106 (BRIM III)

GUIDED PRACTICAL: CREATE AN ACCESSIBLE AUDIO RESOURCE
Suggested time: 70–100 minutes (flexible according to devices).

Purpose
Practise a complete audio information-service workflow, from source protection to an access copy and verified transcript.

Materials
Use the synthetic WAV/MP3/transcript files in assets/practice/, OR record your own 30–60 second authorised narration. Do not record real people or confidential records without appropriate permission.

Steps
1. State a BLISS or BRIM audience and a single communication purpose.
2. Record or import a source WAV. Note filename, duration, sample rate, bit depth, channels and actual file size.
3. Play the full recording and note quality problems.
4. Duplicate or protect the source. Edit only a working version with Audacity or an equivalent editor.
5. Make one justified improvement, such as trimming an artificial delay or reducing constant noise modestly. Do not delete meaningful evidence.
6. Export an access MP3 at a documented bitrate and meaningful filename.
7. Compare playback quality, duration and actual saved file sizes.
8. Prepare or correct the complete narration transcript; check names, numbers and keywords.
9. Create a short metadata record: ID, title, date, narrator/source, language, duration, format, file roles, rights/access.
10. Complete the report template; explain one professional risk and how your approach mitigates it.

Submission
- source or a reference to a permitted source file;
- access derivative (or a permitted demonstration output);
- checked transcript as TXT/PDF;
- one-page metadata + edit log + quality-control report.

Important: where the source recording is restricted, do not submit or upload its sound; use approved synthetic material instead. Use the submission method announced in class.
