Multimedia in Information Services · Unit 05 Assignment
LIU08106 BLISS III / RIU08106 BRIM III

TASK
Produce a short 60–90 second professional video that explains one information-service task; or, where equipment/permission is limited, provide an instructor-approved redesign/evaluation of the synthetic training video.

BLISS: make a library orientation/catalogue tutorial.
BRIM: make a fictional records workflow or authorised archival interpretation video.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS
1. Audience and measurable learning objective.
2. Four-scene storyboard with durations, visuals, narration and a shot list.
3. Edited access MP4 (60–90 seconds) or approved alternative demonstration.
4. Verified WebVTT captions and descriptive transcript.
5. File metadata, edit/version log and permission inventory.
6. Quality/accessibility check and brief mobile/low-bandwidth evaluation.

FORMATIVE RUBRIC /20
Planning/purpose 4; production/editing 5; captions/accessibility 4; metadata/rights/source control 4; QA/reflection 3.

Never upload real personal or restricted records or third-party video without appropriate authorisation. Name synthetic/re-enacted material honestly. Keep original source media separate from exports. A completion button on the website is not an assignment submission; use the official method announced in class.
