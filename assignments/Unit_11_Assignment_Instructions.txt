LIU08106 / RIU08106 — UNIT 11
Legal, Ethical and Inclusive Multimedia Practice

APPLICATION ASSIGNMENT (20 marks — FORMATIVE)
BLISS: Design a publication review and accessible online exhibition for at least five FICTIONAL library photographs, icons and narration files.
BRIM: Design an access and disclosure review for at least five FICTIONAL archival records and oral-history excerpts, including source restrictions and safe derivatives.

REQUIRED EVIDENCE
1. A short statement of audience, purpose and five-item inventory.
2. Rights and licence or permission evidence; identify any unresolved claims honestly.
3. Privacy/consent analysis appropriate to the intended publication, including source versus derivative treatment.
4. At least two accessibility provisions and a keyboard/contrast/media-alternative test log.
5. One responsible-AI decision and a short publication approval or escalation rationale.

RUBRIC /20
Purpose and inventory = 3; rights/attribution evidence = 5; privacy/consent and safe derivatives = 5; inclusive design and tested evidence = 4; AI ethics, decisions and reporting = 3.
