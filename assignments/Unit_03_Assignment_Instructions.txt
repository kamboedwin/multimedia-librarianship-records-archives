LIU08106 / RIU08106 · Unit 03
APPLICATION ASSIGNMENT: RESPONSIBLE IMAGE PUBLICATION

Choose the BLISS or BRIM scenario from the Unit 3 assignment tab. Use only materials created by you, the supplied synthetic practice assets, or images that you are authorised to process and publish. Never upload private records to public services.

Submit, according to the method and deadline announced in class:
1. Source image (or an authorised source reference).
2. Separate edited access derivative with descriptive filename.
3. Editing/processing log: date, inputs, edits, outputs and reasons.
4. One- to two-page explanation of audience, format selection, legibility, rights, OCR suitability and master/derivative practice.
5. Short accessibility and quality-control checklist.
