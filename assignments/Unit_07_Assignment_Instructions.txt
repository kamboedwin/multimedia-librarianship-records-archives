Unit 7 — MULTIMEDIA AUTHORING PROJECT (FORMATIVE)
LIU08106 / RIU08106

BLISS: interactive digital library orientation (search, borrowing and help).
BRIM: fictional interactive archives mini-exhibition (timeline, item context and rights).

Deliverable: working 4–6 screen multimedia prototype with start screen, meaningful navigation, at least one image/audio/video component with appropriate alternatives, and a rights/attribution section.

Required accompanying report (suggested 2–4 pages):
- named audience and two or three measurable user tasks;
- sitemap and screen storyboard;
- brief tool choice and reasoning;
- asset register with source, format, rights and alternative-text / transcript decisions;
- keyboard, navigation, reflow and peer-task testing log;
- one concrete revision and retest; limitations and reference list.

Formative 20-mark rubric:
4: User goals, sitemap, purposeful screen/storyboard structure.
4: Working navigation, meaningful links/actions and recovery paths.
4: Multimedia quality, context, provenance and rights/attribution.
4: Accessibility and task-based usability evidence with fixes.
4: Organisation, documentation, clarity and honest limitations.

Use the official class submission channel and deadline when announced. The site does not upload or collect student work.
