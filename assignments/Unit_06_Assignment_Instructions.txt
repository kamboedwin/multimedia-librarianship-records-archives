Multimedia in Information Services — Unit 6 Application Assignment
Courses: LIU08106 (BLISS III) / RIU08106 (BRIM III)

Goal: propose and test a defensible file-format and delivery workflow for one information resource pack. This is a formative learning task, not necessarily a graded institutional assessment. Follow class announcements for actual submission details.

Choose ONE context:
BLISS — A student digital-library pack: one image containing fine text, a 3-minute catalogue tutorial, a spoken guide and a concise text alternative.
BRIM — A fictional archival exhibition package: one synthetic scanned record, a fictional oral-history snippet and a short annotated video. State source/derivative links, rights and restrictions explicitly.

Required evidence:
1. Audience, purpose and constraints (mobile access, legal conditions, readability, sustainability).
2. Three-row image/audio/video format matrix with at least one controlled source and an access derivative where justified.
3. Measured same-source image comparison of the three supplied fictional images; calculate file ratio and percentage saving for two versions.
4. Correctly labelled raw image or PCM audio estimate and compressed video/transfer estimate, including assumptions.
5. A manifest of files and roles, and at least four release checks: content legibility, playback/compatibility, permissions, captions/transcript, checksum/metadata as relevant.
6. 200–300 word reasoned recommendation and an honest limitation of your tests.

Illustrative practice rubric /20:
Appropriate formats and justified source/access roles 5;
correct calculations and measured compression comparisons 5;
format and delivery matrix, user/device/accessibility considerations 4;
provenance, rights, manifest and checks 4;
clear report and stated limitations 2.
