LIU08106 / RIU08106 | UNIT 02 APPLICATION ASSIGNMENT
TITLE: DESIGN A CLEAR, RESPONSIBLE VISUAL INFORMATION RESOURCE

Choose your programme pathway:
BLISS III: design a one-page digital poster or infographic helping library users
find or use a genuine service (for example, library orientation, e-resources
access or information-literacy event).
BRIM III: design a one-page archival item display or records-management
information card (for example, a mini-exhibition entry with source context).

OUTPUTS
A. One digital design in PDF/PNG format, sized and labelled for an intended
  screen or page; student-generated artwork and/or authorised assets only.
B. A one- to two-page design rationale (PDF or DOCX) including:
  - audience and communication objective;
  - intended medium, dimensions, any relevant PPI reasoning;
  - whether assets are raster or vector and why;
  - colour and typography choices;
  - explanation of hierarchy, alignment, spacing and grouping;
  - accessibility check (legibility, meaningful labels, colour contrast);
  - sources/permissions and, in BRIM, how context and master integrity are
  maintained;
  - brief peer feedback and one implemented improvement.

RECOMMENDED PROCESS
1. Draft a content outline before choosing styles.
2. Sketch two layouts; choose one with a clear reason.
3. Create the design using tools already available to you (e.g. PowerPoint,
  LibreOffice Impress, Canva, or other permitted software).
4. Review it at mobile size and check basic contrast.
5. Document source imagery; do not use restricted records or personal data.

PRACTICE RUBRIC - 20 marks
Problem/audience and required information  4
Technical decisions (dimensions, images, colour)  4
Hierarchy, typography and visual clarity  5
Accessibility and responsible use of source  4
Rationale, peer check and organisation  3
TOTAL  20

Suggested length: design + 1-2 pages of rationale.
