UNIT 1 APPLICATION ASSIGNMENT — MULTIMEDIA SERVICE CONCEPT
LIU08106 / RIU08106 · BLISS III / BRIM III

Goal: Propose a realistic small multimedia resource for your professional field.

BLISS: choose a library/information-service need.
BRIM: choose a records/archives need.

Submit a 2–3 page concept document (excluding diagrams and references) that includes:
1. A project title, the problem to solve and intended users.
2. Two or three user-focused objectives.
3. At least three multimedia elements and why each is appropriate.
4. A simple navigation diagram (linear, non-linear or hybrid).
5. One accessibility measure and one rights/consent precaution.
6. Expected benefits, possible limitations and a way to judge success.

Suggested formative rubric (20 marks):
- Problem, audience and objectives .................. 5
- Media choices and justification ................... 5
- Navigation structure ............................. 4
- Accessibility, rights and risks ................... 4
- Clarity and organisation ......................... 2
TOTAL ............................................. 20
