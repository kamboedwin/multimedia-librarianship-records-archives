Multimedia · Unit 08

Review Questions and Applied Exercises

Attempt these questions before using the learning notes to verify your reasoning.

  1. Define digitisation and explain why it differs from digital preservation.
  2. State four different institutional purposes for digitising analogue material.
  3. Describe a complete eight-stage digitisation workflow.
  4. How can source condition influence digitisation priorities?
  5. What should a digitisation project specification contain?
  6. Explain why a source inventory is needed before scanning.
  7. Why might a blank page need to appear in the manifest?
  8. What handling risks arise when scanning a fragile bound volume?
  9. Compare a flatbed scanner, overhead camera and sheet-fed scanner.
  10. Explain skew, perspective distortion, glare and motion blur.
  11. What is the relationship between original size, PPI and pixel dimensions?
  12. Calculate pixel dimensions for an 8 × 6 inch document at 300 PPI.
  13. Calculate uncompressed 24-bit RGB payload for a 2400 × 1800 pixel image.
  14. Why does increasing PPI not guarantee a better scan?
  15. Give two reasons to capture some records in colour rather than greyscale.
  16. Differentiate capture master, working copy and public derivative.
  17. Propose a naming convention for a five-page fictional record.
  18. Distinguish quality assurance (QA) from quality control (QC).
  19. Describe four visual image QC criteria.
  20. Why must completeness be checked independently of sharpness?
  21. A batch contains 12 expected page IDs but 13 files; suggest two explanations.
  22. Explain the benefits and limits of random QC sampling.
  23. What is OCR, and what kinds of source material challenge it?
  24. Give examples of two character-confusion OCR mistakes.
  25. How can OCR text affect the reliability of full-text searching?
  26. What metadata should be recorded at capture time?
  27. Explain provenance and chain of processing for digitised records.
  28. Why does a file hash not prove image fidelity?
  29. How should confidential material be handled before publication?
  30. Distinguish authorisation to capture and permission to publish.
  31. Design a BLISS workflow to digitise a local-history photo collection.
  32. Design a BRIM workflow for a restricted 12-page records file.
  33. How should a rejected scan be documented and corrected?
  34. Propose a final acceptance checklist for a digitisation batch.

Suggested self-check strategy

For definition questions, state the concept, then give a BLISS or BRIM example. For calculations, show the formula and units. For scenario questions, identify the source, the risks, the decision, the quality check and the evidence recorded. In digitisation planning, a well-justified answer is more useful than memorising one scanner setting.
