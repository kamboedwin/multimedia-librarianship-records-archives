Multimedia Unit 12 — Capstone Starter (fictional content only)

Open index.html in your browser. Three fictional items illustrate identifiers, descriptions, safe access and an accessible user journey. You can customise this starter or use it as reference, but must produce your own original work.

Do not insert personal/confidential institutional records into a public repository. Complete metadata, rights, accessibility and usability templates with your own fictional or authorised materials.

The starter is intentionally simple: no database, no collection-management system, no login or actual restriction enforcement. Its restricted item has NO media download.
